PARP14 CALVADOS Simulation Analysis — Cross-Construct Summary
==============================================================

Six constructs, 25 replicates each (5 AF3 seeds x 5 samples), 20 ns per
replicate (0.5 ns equilibration discarded), 19.5 ns effective per replicate.

Constructs (ordered by chain length):
  md     — MD1L1+MD2+MD3                                   586 res
  mka    — MD1L1+MD2+MD3+KHb-KH8+WWE+ART                  999 res
  core   — KH7a+MD1L1+MD2+MD3+KHb-KH8+WWE+ART            1051 res
  noart  — KH1-6+KH7a+MD1L1+MD2+MD3+KHb-KH8+WWE          1275 res
  norrm  — KH1-6+KH7a+MD1L1+MD2+MD3+KHb-KH8+WWE+ART     1474 res
  fl     — Full-length (all 11 domains)                    1801 res

Key differences:
  - The macrodomains-only construct (md) is compact and well-defined
    (narrow Rg distribution). Adding KHb-KH8+WWE+ART (mka, core)
    roughly doubles the Rg but the chain remains relatively globular.
  - The KH1-6-containing constructs (noart, norrm) are much more
    extended, with Rg comparable despite noart having 200 fewer
    residues than norrm — the KH1-6 block (423 res) is the dominant
    contributor to chain extension.
  - FL is the most extended and variable (Rg 6.6-14.6 nm across
    replicates), reflecting the disordered RRM1-3 N-terminal region
    that can adopt many conformations.


2. DOMAIN COMPACTNESS (Fraction of Native Contacts)
=====================================================

All structured domains maintain high FNC (~0.62-0.67) across all
constructs, confirming that CALVADOS harmonic restraints preserve
domain folds. No domain unfolds in any construct. Variation across
replicates is negligible (std < 0.001).


3. INTERDOMAIN ENERGIES
========================

(kJ/mol, negative = attractive):

  -----------    -----   -----   -----   -----  -----  -----
  MD1-MD2        -8.9    -7.0   -11.3    -8.7   -5.5   -0.9
  MD1-MD3        -3.7    -1.2    -3.2    -4.9   -3.3   -0.4
  MD2-MD3        -2.1    -4.1    -0.4    -1.3   -2.6   -0.3
  MD1-ART         --     -4.7    -5.0     --    -3.2    0.0
  WWE-ART         --     -2.8    -3.4     --    -2.6   -0.6
  KHb-KH8-ART    --     -2.0    -2.7     --    -2.1   -1.1
  MD3-KHb-KH8    --     -1.3    -1.5    -2.3   -0.5   -0.6

Key differences:
  - MD1-MD2 is the strongest interdomain interaction in every
    construct. It is strongest in core (-11.3 kJ/mol), where the
    flanking domains compress the macrodomain triad. In FL, MD1-MD2
    drops to just -0.9 kJ/mol — the extended RRM/KH N-terminus
    pulls the macrodomains apart.
  - Smaller constructs (md, core, mka) have 5-10x stronger
    interdomain interactions than FL, because domains pack more
    tightly without the long disordered linkers.
  - The ART domain consistently forms strong contacts with MD1
    (-3 to -5 kJ/mol) in constructs where it is present, suggesting
    a preferred MD1-ART interface that is disrupted in FL by the
    intervening chain.
  - Electrostatic (Yukawa) contributions are small (< 1 kJ/mol
    per pair) compared to hydrophobic (AH) interactions. The
    interdomain energy landscape is dominated by hydrophobic
    packing.

8. KEY BIOLOGICAL INSIGHTS
============================

(a) The "domain context paradox":
    Removing domains does not simply increase accessibility. The
    relationship is non-monotonic:
    - md (smallest) and FL (largest) have the HIGHEST accessibility
    - core/mka/norrm (intermediate) have the LOWEST accessibility
    Small constructs lack steric blockers. Very large constructs
    extend away from the macrodomain core. Intermediate constructs
    pack flanking domains directly against active sites.

(b) MD1 is the most regulatable site:
    Its accessibility varies 2-fold across constructs (SAA 0.25 in
    norrm to 0.50 in md). MD2 and MD3 are more intrinsically
    buried (high WCN ~12) and less sensitive to domain context.

(c) ART accessibility depends on chain length:
    In FL, ART sits at the chain periphery (radial=1.16) and is
    highly accessible (SAA=0.58). In shorter constructs, ART is
    pulled toward the interior and partially occluded.

(d) MD1-MD2 is the critical structural interface:
    It is the strongest interdomain interaction in every construct
    (-0.9 to -11.3 kJ/mol), driven entirely by hydrophobic (AH)
    packing. This interface is strongest when the construct is
    compact and weakest in FL.

(e) The KHb-KH8/WWE/ART C-terminal module:
    These three domains form a consistent structural unit with high
    contact frequencies and strong energies across all constructs.
    The KHb-KH8-WWE interface is the most persistent contact in the
    entire protein.

(f) Electrostatics play a minor role:
    Yukawa contributions are <1 kJ/mol per domain pair in all
    constructs. The interdomain energy landscape is dominated by
    hydrophobic (Ashbaugh-Hatch) interactions.
